Exhibit B

How a look flows

Turn an authorized walkthrough into a documented asset debrief. Every price carries its source; value ranks assets for review, not proven security risk. Visibility alone does not prove an asset is unsecured.

  1. 01

    The gate

    The camera waits for a new view. A flicker or a passing hand does not count. A different shelf does.

  2. 02

    The look

    The hat identifies the object and marks its place in the room. Each find gets a name and a category. An offline catalog keeps the demo going when the connection drops.

  3. 03

    The appraisal

    Sold listings come first, then a model’s quote, then a visual estimate. Every price tells you where it came from. Estimates are marked clearly.

  4. 04

    The ledger

    One shared ledger keeps the crew in sync and filters out repeat finds. Each entry records who spotted it, its value, and the evidence behind the appraisal.

  5. 05

    The callout

    When a find makes the top five, the speaker reads it out and its marker flashes on the projector. The wrist picks up the new top five on its next poll.

  6. 06

    The reveal

    The button on the cap freezes the frame and opens the debrief: the five highest-value observed assets, a total, one stamp. The QR opens the case file on a phone. Press it again and the next assessment opens.
