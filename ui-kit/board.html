ToThe judges FromThe crew ReCase No. 1138, The Appraisal Job

A camera on a cap prices whatever is in the room.

The screen is the paperwork. Each find is an exhibit. The price is an appraisal. The top five is a lineup. The page you scan is a stamped case file.

One rule. If it would not sit in a 1950s police station, or on 1950s film, it is not on screen.

  1. The camera sends a still every couple of seconds. Each one lands as a hard cut.
  2. While the model thinks, the ledger types EXAMINING EXHIBIT 07. A pause should look like work.
  3. A guessed price says est. on every screen.
  4. Failures stay in the film. Footage lost. Line dead. A vacant slot. Case unsolved.
  5. Academy leader at the start. A cue mark before the reveal. The End when the case closes.
  6. Red is stamp ink. Everything else is black, white, and the greys between.
