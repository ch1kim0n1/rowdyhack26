What to watch

  • Frame analysis threads the film and shows elapsed time—not an invented percentage. Leave it running for ten seconds to see the slow-response state.
  • A finding gets one paper notification. Repeated snapshots don't replay it.
  • Connection and camera failures keep existing evidence on screen. Reconnect only announces after a confirmed snapshot.
  • Actions show sending, wait for the response, and only then confirm. Failure restores the control.
